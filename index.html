Font sizes and units

  1. The quick brown fox jumped over the lazy dog.
    • Pixels: px is an absolute unit, it allows full precise control and equal font sizes across devices
  2. The quick brown fox jumped over the lazy dog.
    • Points:
  3. The quick brown fox jumped over the lazy dog.
    • Percentages:
  4. The quick brown fox jumped over the lazy dog.
    • rem: rem is a unit that scales relative to the font size at the root html element. For example, if the root has a defined font size of 16px, and I define a size in the child element of 0.5rem, my font will be 8px.
  5. The quick brown fox jumped over the lazy dog.
    • em: em is a unit that works similarly to rem, however instead of scaling relative to the root element, it scales relative to the parent element.
